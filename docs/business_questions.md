# Business Questions

This document defines the business questions that the AI Sales Analytics Agent should be able to answer using the sales analytics database.

The questions are used as test scenarios for the AI agent.

---

# 1. Revenue & Sales

## Q1. Total Revenue

**Question:**

What is the total revenue?

**Expected analysis:**

Calculate the total amount of completed sales.

**Relevant tables:**

- sales

**Relevant columns:**

- sales.amount
- sales.status

---

## Q2. Revenue by Month

**Question:**

What is the revenue by month?

**Expected analysis:**

Calculate total revenue grouped by month using the sale date.

**Relevant tables:**

- sales

**Relevant columns:**

- sales.amount
- sales.sale_date
- sales.status

---

# 5. Lead Follow-up & Sales Activity

## Q18. Leads Without Activities

**Question:**

Which leads have no activities?

**Expected analysis:**

Find leads that do not have any related activity records.

**Relevant tables:**

- leads
- activities

**Relevant columns:**

- leads.lead_id
- leads.created_at
- activities.lead_id

---

## Q19. Leads Not Contacted Within 24 Hours

**Question:**

Which leads were not contacted within 24 hours?

**Expected analysis:**

Compare lead creation time with the first related activity and identify leads where the first contact happened after 24 hours or no contact activity exists.

**Relevant tables:**

- leads
- activities

**Relevant columns:**

- leads.lead_id
- leads.created_at
- activities.lead_id
- activities.activity_type
- activities.activity_date

**Note:**

The current database stores activity dates but does not explicitly store a separate "first contact" timestamp.

---

## Q20. High-Value Leads Without Activity

**Question:**

Which high-value leads have not been contacted?

**Expected analysis:**

Find leads with a high estimated value that have no relevant contact activity.

**Relevant tables:**

- leads
- activities

**Relevant columns:**

- leads.lead_id
- leads.estimated_value
- leads.status
- activities.lead_id
- activities.activity_type

**Note:**

The definition of "high-value" should be determined by the AI or specified by the user, for example, leads with estimated value above a given threshold.
